# Problem statement alignment

Challenge 6, Urban Air Quality Assistant: help families and communities get through severe winter smog with low-emission routines, indoor air hacks and sustainable alternatives tailored to location and lifestyle, and clearly explain the impact of each choice.

| The brief asks for          | Saans delivers                                                                                                         |
| --------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Location                    | City search, browser location, quick picks for smog-hit cities; live Indian AQI, US AQI and a 72-hour forecast         |
| Lifestyle                   | Household profile: people, sensitive members, commute, cooking fuel, heating, purifier/AC, room size, WFH days, budget |
| Low-emission daily routines | Today's plan: cleanest hour outside, windows, masks by band, commute, cooking, cleaning                                |
| Indoor air hacks            | Windows, purifier or DIY fan filter, no incense, exhaust while frying, wet mopping, each with an estimate              |
| Sustainable alternatives    | One switch per household, with CO2 saved per day and week                                                              |
| Explain the impact          | Dose cut against doing nothing, with formulas, assumptions and sources on screen                                       |
| Families and communities    | English and Hindi, safety wording for sensitive members, accessible on low-end phones, free to run                     |
